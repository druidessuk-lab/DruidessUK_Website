About "Trip Meadow"

Release Date: Friday 26th June 2026

Genre: Heavy Psych / Doom / Proto-Metal

Format: Vinyl / CD / Digital

Trip Meadow is the debut album from Newcastle-upon-Tyne's Druidess. The band deliver a visceral sonic cocktail: Iommi-worshipping riffs, ethereal vocals with the grit of Joan Jett, the haunting psychedelic textures of Ray Manzarek - all propelled by the Dale Crover school of heavy-hitting percussion.

Since the release of their 2024 EP Hermits and Mandrakes, Druidess have become a familiar face in the UK doom and stoner scenes, with appearances at the likes of Masters of the Riff, Byker Grave Festival, Tonehenge, Cosmic Vibration Festival and Fuzzness under their belt.

They have shared stages with Castle Rat, Slomatics, Dopethrone and Margarita Witch Cult, with a confirmed feature in Metal Hammer magazine supporting previous coverage from the likes of Powerplay Magazine, The Obelisk and Outlaws of the Sun.
